DREAMS Laboratory

arizona state university

The Distributed Robotic Exploration and Mapping Systems (DREAMS) Laboratory at Arizona State University builds robotic systems and digital twins for mapping, sampling, and analysis in terrestrial, aquatic, and planetary environments. DeepGIS is developed in the lab.

  • Terrestrial

    Ground and aerial robots and fixed sensors for geomorphology, drylands ecology, and agriculture.

  • Aquatic

    Robotic boats and underwater vehicles for mapping and sampling lakes, coasts, and reefs.

  • Cyber-Physical Twins

    Simulated and physical counterparts of robots and field sites, used to study and predict environmental processes.

  • Planetary

    Surface feature mapping and digital twins of the Moon and Mars.
